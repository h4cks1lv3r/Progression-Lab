# Product and Training Research

## Program model

The supplied 129-screen Kindle sequence was indexed in full and 62
representative pages were visually reviewed. It contains 3-, 4-, and 5-day
variants organized into phases
and repeating 16-microcycle blocks. The implementation preserves the observed
structure: mostly four exercises, four hard sets in loading weeks, planned
volume deloads, heavier primary-lift prescriptions over the block, an AMRAP
strength week, and a two-set full deload. Phase 1's five-day pages were not
present, so that specific template remains unverified.

Mike Matthews describes Bigger Leaner Stronger as a strength-and-muscle plan
centered on compound lifting and progressive overload. His public guidance on
double progression recommends selecting a rep range, improving reps before
adding load, and generally ending hard sets with roughly one or two reps in
reserve. Sources:

- https://legionathletics.com/bigger-leaner-stronger-workout-plan/
- https://legionathletics.com/double-progression/
- https://legionathletics.com/what-is-progressive-overload/

## Stacked workflow

The public App Store listing emphasizes fast planning and workout logging.
Contemporary reviews identify the useful interaction model: previous workout
data, PRs, estimated 1RM, rest timer, numeric entry, plate calculator, workout
playlists, and included BLS routines. Reliability, export, and duplicated or
lost data were recurring gaps, so Progression Lab is local-first and its program
calendar is generated deterministically.

- https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stacked-workout-tracker-for/id1116989938
- https://www.lifewire.com/best-workout-log-apps-4140222

## Competitive requirements

Strong, Hevy, FitNotes, Jefit, Fitbod, and HeavySet converge on a few
bodybuilding essentials: minimal-tap logging, previous-set recall, rest timing,
exercise substitution, plate math, routine scheduling, charts, volume/1RM
trends, cloud or export safety, and wearable-friendly controls. Progression Lab's
first Android release prioritizes the gym-critical subset while leaving clear
extension points for backup/export, Health Connect, media controls, and a
wearable companion.
